About
ZShoot

ZShoot is an arcade shooter built to fit in a pocket. One thumb aims, one thumb fires, and the waves do not wait.

Version 3 grew the game with a shop, an inventory, daily rewards, eighteen guns and eighteen worlds, but the idea stayed the same: keep the loop tight, keep the game light, and let the player decide how far they can push it. There is no account to make and no internet to find. You install it, turn the phone sideways and start shooting.

The game is built and updated by one developer, and the roadmap on this page is open to players. What you send under Shape ZShoot is read by a person.
