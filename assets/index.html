Quick answers

Does ZShoot need internet?

No. The app does not request internet access, so you can play anywhere and the game has no way to send data off your device.

Why does Android warn me before installing?

Android shows that warning for every app installed from a file instead of an app store. Compare the SHA-256 checksum in the download section with your file if you want to confirm it is the official build.

Which phones can run it?

Any phone or tablet on Android 5.0 or newer. The game plays in landscape and keeps the screen awake while you play.

How do I get new guns and worlds?

Earn coins by playing, then buy them in the shop. Five rare guns and five worlds also come as daily rewards, so keep claiming.

How do the daily rewards work?

Open Daily Rewards from the home screen and tap the card for the current day. You can claim once a day, and the calendar runs for 30 days.

Where is my progress saved?

On your device only: coins, owned and equipped items, settings, stats and your best wave. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling it removes them.

How do I control the game?

Hold the phone sideways. Your left thumb aims, your right thumb fires.

How do I follow my ticket or idea?

Open My requests at the top of the page. The status, the developer's reply and the full conversation are there, and a badge shows when something new arrives.

Can I delete what I sent?

Email the support address with the reference code from My requests and it will be removed. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
